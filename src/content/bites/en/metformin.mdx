## Why does it happen?

Metformin has several effects within the gastrointestinal tract. These effects can contribute to symptoms such as nausea, abdominal discomfort and diarrhea.

## A question worth exploring

The interesting part is that the exact mechanisms are more complicated than simply saying that metformin "irritates the stomach."
